# Ownsonic icon

The launcher mark combines lime headphones and a pale green Play symbol on the app's dark green background. `ownsonic-icon.svg` is the editable artwork, and `ownsonic-icon-preview.png` is its preview.

Android uses the vector foreground and background in `app/src/main/res/drawable` through the adaptive icon resources. The foreground is also used as the monochrome layer for themed launcher icons. The mark stays within the adaptive icon's central safe area. Density-specific WebP resources provide matching rounded-square and circular fallback icons.

The player rating icons `ic_like.xml` and `ic_dislike.xml` are adaptations of Google's Material Icons `thumb_up` design under Apache-2.0. Ownsonic simplifies the coordinates and rotates the dislike variant. Attribution and license texts are included in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt` and `legal/` at the repository root.
